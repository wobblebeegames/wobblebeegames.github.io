Privacy Policy

Nuts & Bolts: Color Sort

This policy explains what information Nuts & Bolts: Color Sort ("the game") handles, why, and the choices you have. The game is published by WobbleBee Games. Last updated: November 1, 2026.

What we collect

We do not run servers and we do not collect your name, email or contacts. Your progress (levels, coins, stars, cars, settings) is stored on your device. If Android backup is on, Android may back this game data up to your own Google account so it can be restored on a new device.

Google Play Games

If you use Google Play Games, the game sends your achievements, leaderboard scores, game statistics (such as levels won and nuts sorted) and a copy of your progress to Google Play Games, so you can see them in your Play Games profile and continue on another device. This is linked to your Play Games profile and handled under Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy. You can sign out or manage your Play Games data in the Google Play Games app.

App analytics and crash reports

To find bugs and improve the game, it may use Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics. They receive how the game is used (for example levels started and won, features used), crash reports and device information (model, Android version) with an app-instance identifier, not your name or contacts. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, analytics only runs if you allowed storing information on your device in the consent form. This data is processed by Google as our service provider: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Advertising

The game shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland we ask for your consent before ads are personalized; you can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Learn how Google uses this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the game schedules local notifications on your device: at most one a day (the daily puzzle, gift or event), and a reminder if you have not played for a few days. No data leaves your device for this, and you can turn reminders off in Settings or in your Android settings at any time.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deleting data

You can change ad and analytics consent in Settings > Privacy choices, reset your advertising ID in Android settings, and delete all game data on your device by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the game. Data in Google Play Games can be deleted from the Play Games app.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version. Questions? Contact us at wobblebee.games@gmail.com.
